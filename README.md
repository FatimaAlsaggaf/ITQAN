# Itqan | إتقان

An AI-powered workforce intelligence platform for skills-based team formation, employee development, and knowledge continuity.

![Itqan workforce intelligence interface](https://fatima-alsaggaf-portfolio.vercel.app/images/projects/itqan.png)

## The problem

Organizations need to understand the skills they already have before assigning teams, planning development, or hiring. Gaps in that understanding can leave expertise underused and critical knowledge concentrated in a small number of people.

## The idea

Itqan brings employee skills, experience, availability, and project requirements together to support workforce decisions. It helps organizations form teams, identify skill gaps, recommend development opportunities, and reduce reliance on individual knowledge holders.

## Features

- **AI team formation:** Recommend teams using employee capabilities and project needs.
- **Explainable recommendations:** Make the reasoning behind recommendations easier to understand.
- **Skill gap analysis:** Identify missing capabilities and development opportunities.
- **Cost-benefit analysis:** Support comparisons between workforce planning options.
- **Knowledge risk detection:** Highlight dependencies on individual employees.
- **Employee development:** Support growth opportunities for employees and trainees.
- **Shadowing and knowledge transfer:** Help preserve expertise across the organization.
